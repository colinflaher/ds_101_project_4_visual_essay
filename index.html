Project Overview

Our reddit sentiment analysis is the comparison of crime rate in Richmond, Virginia near VCU versus Harrisonburg, Virginia near JMU. Our project is showing the emotions toward nearby locations near VCU and JMU based on student reddit posts. Since VCU is located in a major city, Richmond, VA, our hypothesis states crime rate will be higher than near JMU, which is not located in a major city. Harrisonburg, VA is a much more rural and less populated area than Richmond is. We predicted that students at JMU would have a lot less crime exposure due to a more rural location than a major city. There is three popular spots at JMU and VCU that will be shown on the interactive tour that are frequently discussed on reddit by students. All locations are primarily found on campus, but some are not directly on campus. Starting off with JMU, you will see the Quad, Village dorms, and Harrisonburg City. The Quad and Village dorms are directly on campus. Harrisonburg City is off campus, but it is not a huge city and it is very close and accessible for students to go visit. For VCU, you will see Monroe Park, Richmond, and GRC. Monroe Park and GRC are located directly on VCU campus. Richmond is the city VCU is located in. It is a very densely populated city with many surrounding areas and locations for students and closeby citizens to visit.
